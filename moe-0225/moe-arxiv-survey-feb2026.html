The most intellectually active area this week. Three papers propose fundamentally new routing paradigms that move away from the standard Top-k + Softmax approach, each bringing a distinct mathematical framework.本周学术活跃度最高的方向。三篇论文提出了从根本上不同于标准 Top-k + Softmax 的新路由范式，各自引入了独特的数学框架。

Feb 192月19日 GrMoE: Grassmannian Mixture-of-Experts (arXiv: 2602.17798)GrMoE: 格拉斯曼流形上的 MoE (arXiv: 2602.17798) — **Shihab, Akter, Sharma** — Formalizes the connection between MoE routing and classification on the **Grassmannian manifold**. Derives a new gating mechanism from the **Matrix Bingham distribution**, replacing discrete top-k selection with a smooth, geometrically principled sparsity mechanism. The "concentration matrix" provides an interpretable knob for controlling routing entropy. Results: **zero routing collapse** across 350M–2.7B models, with **15–30% improved load balance** and comparable or better perplexity. Experts learn heterogeneous concentration values correlated with linguistic specialization, enabling **post-hoc sparsity tuning without retraining**.**Shihab, Akter, Sharma** — 将 MoE 路由与**格拉斯曼流形**上的分类问题建立了形式化联系。从 **Matrix Bingham 分布**推导出新的门控机制，用平滑的几何稀疏机制取代离散的 top-k 选择。「浓度矩阵」提供了一个可解释的旋钮来控制路由熵。结果：在 350M–2.7B 模型上实现**零路由坍塌**，**负载均衡提升 15–30%**，困惑度持平或更优。各专家学到的浓度值与语言特化相关，支持**无需重训练的事后稀疏度调节**。
  2. Feb 192月19日 PA-MoE: Phase-Aware MoE for Agentic RL (arXiv: 2602.17038)PA-MoE: 面向智能体 RL 的阶段感知 MoE (arXiv: 2602.17038) — **Yang, Li, He, Li, Cai, Jiang, Feng** (Southeast Univ. & Kuaishou) — Identifies the **simplicity bias** problem in RL policy networks where easy tasks dominate parameter updates. Proposes a **lightweight phase router** that learns latent phase boundaries directly from the RL objective (no predefined categories), allocating **temporally consistent** expert assignments. Each expert preserves phase-specific expertise, preventing catastrophic interference between task phases.**Yang, Li, He, Li, Cai, Jiang, Feng**（东南大学 & 快手）— 揭示了 RL 策略网络中的**简单性偏差**问题：简单任务霸占了大部分参数更新。提出**轻量级阶段路由器**，直接从 RL 目标学习潜在阶段边界（无需预定义类别），实现**时间一致性**的专家分配。每个专家保持阶段特定的专长，防止任务阶段间的灾难性干扰。
  3. Feb 232月23日 Replicate-and-Quantize: Plug-and-Play Load Balancing (arXiv: 2602.19938)复制-量化策略：即插即用负载均衡 (arXiv: 2602.19938) — **Liu, Peng, Duan et al.** — A **training-free** inference-time framework for dynamically rebalancing expert workloads. Key insight: expert importance cannot be reliably determined by selection frequency alone. Proposes **replicating heavy-hitter experts** to increase throughput capacity while **quantizing underutilized experts** to maintain memory budget. Introduces Load-Imbalance Score (LIS) metric. Achieves **up to 1.4x load imbalance reduction** while maintaining accuracy within ±0.6%.**Liu, Peng, Duan 等** — 一种**无需训练**的推理时框架，用于动态重新平衡专家负载。核心洞察：专家重要性不能仅凭选择频率来判断。提出**复制高负载专家**以增加吞吐能力，同时**量化低利用率专家**以维持内存预算。引入负载不平衡评分 (LIS) 指标。实现**最高 1.4 倍负载不平衡降低**，精度偏差在 ±0.6% 以内。

*Trend Analysis & Observations趋势分析与观察

1. Beyond Top-k + Softmax Routing

The standard Top-k + Softmax routing paradigm is being actively challenged. This week alone, GrMoE (Grassmannian/Bingham), DirMoE (Dirichlet VAE, ICLR 2026), and PA-MoE (phase-aware temporal routing) each propose fundamentally different mathematical foundations for expert selection. The trend is clear: the community is moving toward geometrically principled and probabilistically grounded routing that provides better theoretical guarantees against expert collapse.

2. Training-Free Inference Optimization

A notable theme is training-free methods for MoE inference: MoE-Spec (expert budgeting), Replicate-and-Quantize (load rebalancing), and the in-memory computing work all operate at inference time without model retraining. This reflects the practical reality that many organizations deploy pre-trained MoE models (DeepSeek-V3, Qwen-MoE) and need optimization without access to training infrastructure.

3. Expert Specialization Remains Unsolved

SD-MoE's spectral analysis confirms what practitioners have long suspected: expert specialization frequently fails, with experts becoming functionally redundant. The paper's finding that dominant gradient subspaces are strongly aligned across experts provides a principled explanation. Expect more work on encouraging genuine expert differentiation.

4. MoE Systems at Scale

Two papers accepted at top systems venues (LAER-MoE at ASPLOS 2026, DeepFusion at AAMAS 2026) signal that MoE parallelism and distributed training are reaching production maturity. The FSEP paradigm from LAER-MoE (1.69x speedup) is particularly noteworthy for its dynamic expert re-layout capability.

5. Information-Theoretic Foundations

The rate-distortion analysis of MoE gating (2602.15091) and the hyperspherical geometry discovery in MoE embeddings (2602.14039) suggest the field is developing stronger theoretical underpinnings. These results could eventually inform principled architecture design rather than empirical trial-and-error.

1. 超越 Top-k + Softmax 路由

标准的 Top-k + Softmax 路由范式正在被积极挑战。仅本周就有 GrMoE（格拉斯曼/Bingham 分布）、DirMoE（Dirichlet VAE，ICLR 2026）和 PA-MoE（阶段感知时序路由）分别提出了全新的数学基础。趋势很明确：社区正在转向几何原理化和概率论基础化的路由方案，以提供更好的理论保证来对抗专家坍塌。

2. 无需训练的推理优化

一个显著主题是 MoE 推理的免训练方法：MoE-Spec（专家预算）、复制-量化策略（负载重平衡）和存内计算工作都在推理阶段运作，无需模型重训练。这反映了现实：许多组织部署的是预训练好的 MoE 模型（DeepSeek-V3、Qwen-MoE），需要在没有训练基础设施的情况下进行优化。

3. 专家特化仍未解决

SD-MoE 的谱分析证实了从业者长期以来的怀疑：专家特化经常失败，专家变得功能冗余。论文发现主导梯度子空间在各专家间强对齐，提供了原理性解释。预计会有更多关于促进真正专家差异化的工作。

4. 大规模 MoE 系统

两篇被顶级系统会议接收的论文（LAER-MoE @ ASPLOS 2026、DeepFusion @ AAMAS 2026）表明 MoE 并行和分布式训练正在走向生产成熟。LAER-MoE 的 FSEP 范式（1.69 倍加速）因其动态专家重布局能力特别值得关注。

5. 信息论基础

MoE 门控的率失真分析 (2602.15091) 和 MoE 嵌入中超球面几何的发现 (2602.14039) 表明该领域正在发展更强的理论基础。这些结果最终可能指导有原则的架构设计，而非经验性的反复试验。
