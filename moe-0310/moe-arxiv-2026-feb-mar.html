Spotlight: ERC Loss (ICLR 2026 Oral)重点关注：ERC Loss（ICLR 2026 Oral）
The Expert-Router Coupling (ERC) loss proposes a lightweight auxiliary loss that ensures router embeddings faithfully represent expert capabilities. Key innovation: operates on only n² activations (n = number of experts), making it independent of batch size. Demonstrated effectiveness on 3B–15B parameter MoE-LLMs trained on trillions of tokens. This represents a shift from architectural changes to training-objective-level solutions for expert specialization.ERC Loss 提出了一种轻量辅助损失，确保路由嵌入忠实反映专家能力。核心创新：仅对 n² 个激活值操作（n 为专家数），计算量与 batch 大小无关。在 3B 到 15B 参数的 MoE-LLM 上验证了有效性。这标志着专家特化方案从架构层面改动转向训练目标层面优化。
